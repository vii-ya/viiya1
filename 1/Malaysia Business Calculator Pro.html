17. Formula Reference

The calculator is based on standard management-accounting relationships.
Variable Cost / Unit
Product Cost + Packaging + Shipping + Commission + Warranty + Other Variable + Percentage Fees + Refund Allowance

Contribution / Unit
Selling Price − Variable Cost / Unit

Contribution Margin
Contribution / Unit ÷ Selling Price × 100

Break-even Units
Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution / Unit
Break-even Revenue
Break-even Units × Selling Price

Target Margin Price
Fixed Variable Cost ÷ (1 − Percentage Fee − Refund − Target Margin)

Target Profit Price
(Fixed Cost + Desired Profit) ÷ Units + Fixed Variable Cost

Payback
Recommended Capital ÷ Monthly Net Profit
